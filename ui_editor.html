Shortcuts

Drag to move or resize. Arrow keys: 1 px; Shift + arrows: 10 px. Shift + click or drag on empty space: multiple selection. ⌘G group, ⌘⇧G ungroup, ⌘A select all. ⌘C / ⌘V copy and paste layers (also between screens); ⇧⌘R paste to replace the selected layers (same place, parent, cell and order), ⌥⇧⌘R also takes their size. Alt + drag: duplicate; Alt + click: layer underneath; ⌘ while dragging: no snapping; Shift while drawing a line or dragging one of its ends: 0° / 45° / 2:1 iso. A line is selected by its pixels; drag its round handles to move its ends (Length / Angle in Properties). Constraints (Properties): per axis Left, Right, Left & Right, Centre or Scale, kept when moving or resizing a layer or its parent; Alt + click on an alignment icon sets the matching constraint. Delete removes, [ ] restack, = / − zoom. Dropping a layer inside a container makes it a child; drawing starts inside the container under the pointer. Shortcuts work outside text fields. Double-click a text to edit it on the canvas (Enter: done, Esc: cancel). O draws an ellipse (Shift: circle). X swaps black / white of the selection (with children). Resize: Shift keeps proportions, Alt resizes from the centre. Pan: Space + drag, middle button or drag the margin; ⌘ + wheel zooms. Layers: drag rows to reorder, drop on the middle of a container to nest. Panels: drag their inner edge. A layer dropped into a container whose background is its own colour flips black / white to stay visible (undo restores it). Iso box: drag its orange diamonds to change A, B and H.

Widgets

Saved sets of layers, shared by every project (widgets/ in the OctaDraw work folder). Click to insert: into the selected container if it fits, else centred on the screen. Save a widget from a selection (Properties → Save as widget).
